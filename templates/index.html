Who's abusing your free trial?

Upload your signup list and see which accounts are probably the same person or throwaway signups — with a plain-English reason for every flag.

What it checks

  • Gmail dot and +tag aliases that land in one inbox
  • Several signups from one IP in 24 hours
  • The same browser on the same IP
  • Disposable email domains
  • Numbered aliases (name1@, name2@, name3@)
  • Your own device / fingerprint id, if you have one

What it needs

Only an email column is required. Add IP, user agent, signup date or a device id column for stronger checks. Column names are detected automatically.

Private by design

Your list is analyzed in memory and never stored. No account needed.
